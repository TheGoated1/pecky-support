Privacy policy

Pecky uses the minimum information needed to run a private, shared meal-planning service. Pecky does not sell personal data and does not contain advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information Pecky processes

Pecky processes account identifiers, display names, household membership, meal and recipe content, photos, preferences, calendar plans, grocery lists, assignments and notification tokens. Preferences may include allergies or foods a person does not want suggested; Pecky does not ask for a medical history.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, synchronise household plans, generate grocery lists, remember preferences, deliver opted-in notifications, protect free-plan limits and restore subscription access. A local cache is kept on the device to make the app responsive.

Sharing and service providers

Household planning information is visible to members of the same household. Pecky uses Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Expo for optional push notification delivery; RevenueCat and the relevant app store for purchase status. These services process information only to provide their contracted functions.

Photos and imported recipes

Photos selected for a shared meal or household are uploaded to private storage and made available to household members through time-limited links. When a recipe URL is imported, Pecky requests that public page to read recipe data exposed by the publisher.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while an account is active. You can begin permanent deletion from Settings in Pecky. When another member remains, shared household content may remain and ownership is transferred; the departing member’s account and private data are removed. Purchase records may remain with Apple, Google or RevenueCat under their own legal obligations.

Your choices

You can decline notifications, edit household content, remove meal photos and delete your account in the app. For another privacy request, use the support route below without posting sensitive information in a public issue.

Security and children

Pecky uses authenticated access and row-level database controls to isolate households. No internet service can promise absolute security. Pecky is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Use the Pecky support page for privacy questions. Account deletion is available directly in Settings and does not require contacting support.

This policy may be updated when Pecky’s features or service providers change. Material changes will be dated on this page.
