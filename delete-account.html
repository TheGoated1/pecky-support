ACCOUNT HELP

Delete your Pecky account

Account deletion is available inside Pecky and does not require a support request.

  1. Open Settings in Pecky.
  2. Scroll to the account section and choose Delete account.
  3. Read the household impact, then confirm both prompts.

What happens

Your profile, membership and private account data are permanently removed. If another household member remains, Pecky transfers shared records and household ownership so their plans are not erased. A Pecky Plus purchase is not transferred to another App Store account.

Before deleting

Deletion cannot be undone. Ask another household member to retain any shared recipes or plans they need. Subscription cancellation is managed separately through your App Store account.
