Terms of use

Pecky helps households choose meals, share recipes and organise groceries. These terms describe the basic rules for using the service.

Accounts and households

You are responsible for your sign-in account and for sharing household invitation codes carefully. Household members can see and change shared household content according to the controls available in Pecky.

Food and allergy information

Pecky is a planning tool, not medical, nutrition or food-safety advice. Imported and user-created recipes may be incomplete. Always verify ingredients, allergens, preparation and storage requirements yourself.

Your content

You keep ownership of recipes, notes and photos you add. You grant Pecky permission to store, process and display that content solely to operate and improve the service for you and your household. Only upload content you have the right to use.

Pecky Plus

Pecky Plus may be offered monthly, yearly or as a lifetime purchase. Current prices and trial terms appear in the App Store purchase sheet before confirmation. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled through the relevant store account. Restore Purchases and subscription management are available in the app.

Availability and acceptable use

Features may change and occasional interruptions may occur. Do not attempt to access another household without permission, disrupt the service, scrape private data or use Pecky unlawfully.

Ending use

You may stop using Pecky or delete your account from Settings. Pecky may restrict abusive or unlawful use where reasonably necessary to protect people or the service.

Questions can be raised through the Pecky support page.
