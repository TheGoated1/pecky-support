POPULAR QUESTIONS

Small fixes, clearly explained.

My household is not syncing

Check that both people joined the same household and have an internet connection. Pull away from the screen and reopen Pecky to trigger a refresh. If a change still does not appear, sign out and back in before reporting it.

How do I change a planned meal?

Open Calendar, tap the three dots beside the meal and choose Change meal. If your household locks swiped meals, Pecky sends a request to the person who planned that week.

How do shared groceries work?

Choose a week or date range, select the meals to include, then update the list. Long-press a meal or grocery item to assign it. Ticked items sync to the household and settle at the bottom.

How do I restore Pecky Plus?

Open Settings, choose Pecky Plus, then use Restore purchases. Purchases belong to the store account and unlock the active Pecky household.

How do I leave or delete an account?

Open Settings and choose Delete account. Pecky asks twice before permanently deleting the account. Shared household ownership is transferred when another member remains.

A recipe import missed something

Some recipe sites do not expose structured recipe data. Paste the ingredients and method into the fallback fields, then edit each ingredient card before saving.

STILL PECKISH FOR AN ANSWER?

Tell us what went wrong.

Open a support request with the device model, iOS version and the steps that produced the problem. Do not include passwords, invitation codes or private recipe links.
